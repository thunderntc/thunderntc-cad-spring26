About Recipe Share

Project Overview

Recipe Share is a test project created for Collaborative Application Development (Spring 2026). This application serves as a learning platform for students to practice collaborative development, version control, and web application design.

Our Mission

Recipe Share is a community-driven platform dedicated to connecting NTC students through the universal language of food. We believe that sharing recipes is more than just sharing ingredients and instructions—it's about sharing culture, memories, and traditions.

What We Do

Our platform empowers NTC students to:

  • Share their favorite recipes with a supportive community
  • Discover new dishes and cooking techniques from peers
  • Rate and review recipes to help others find culinary gems
  • Connect with fellow food enthusiasts and build lasting friendships

Why Recipe Share?

In an increasingly digital world, we believe food has the power to bring people together. Whether you're a seasoned chef or just learning to cook, Recipe Share provides a welcoming space to explore, learn, and grow. Every recipe shared is a story told, every review given is a connection made.

Our Community

Recipe Share is built by and for NTC students. We're committed to fostering an inclusive, respectful, and creative community where everyone's culinary contributions are valued and celebrated.

Educational Purpose

This project is part of the Collaborative Application Development course for Spring 2026, where students learn and practice:

  • Git and GitHub workflows
  • Collaborative development practices
  • Issue tracking and project management
  • Front-end web development
  • Team communication and coordination

Get Involved

Ready to share your culinary creations? Head over to our Recipes page to submit your favorite dishes or explore what others have shared!
